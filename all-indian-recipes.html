Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani) – Punjab

Ingredients:

  • 500g boneless chicken
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup tomato puree
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp chili powder, salt to taste
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tbsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
  • 1/2 cup yogurt, 1 onion (chopped)

Cooking Process:

  1. Marinate chicken with yogurt, half the spices, salt, and ginger garlic paste for 30min+.
  2. Grill or pan-cook the chicken till cooked through.
  3. In a pan, heat butter, sauté onions, add tomato puree, cook till oil separates.
  4. Add remaining spices, kasuri methi, cream. Simmer for 5 min.
  5. Add chicken pieces, simmer 10 min, garnish with cream. Serve hot.

Chole Bhature – Punjab/Delhi

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup dried chickpeas
  • 2 onions, 2 tomatoes, 2 green chilies
  • Spices: cumin, coriander, garam masala, amchur, turmeric, chili powder
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour, 1/2 cup yogurt, oil (for frying)

Cooking Process:

  1. Soak and boil chickpeas. Sauté onions, tomatoes, spices, add chickpeas and simmer.
  2. Knead dough with flour, yogurt, rest 2 hrs. Roll and deep fry bhature.
  3. Serve chole with hot bhature and salad.

Dal Makhani – Punjab

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup whole urad dal (black lentils), 1/4 cup rajma (kidney beans)
  • 2 tomatoes, 1 onion, ginger, garlic, green chili
  • Butter, cream, cumin, garam masala, chili powder, salt

Cooking Process:

  1. Soak and pressure cook lentils/beans. Sauté onions, tomatoes, spices. Add cooked dal, simmer with butter and cream for 30+ min.
  2. Garnish with more cream. Serve with naan or rice.

Masala Dosa – Karnataka

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups rice, 1/2 cup urad dal, 1/4 cup chana dal
  • Salt, oil (for dosa)
  • Potato masala: 4 potatoes, 1 onion, 2 green chilies, curry leaves, mustard seeds, turmeric

Cooking Process:

  1. Soak rice and dals overnight. Grind to smooth batter, ferment 8 hours.
  2. Cook potatoes, mash. Sauté onions, chilies, curry leaves, mustard seeds, turmeric, add potatoes.
  3. Heat tawa, pour batter, spread thin, drizzle oil, cook crisp.
  4. Put masala in center, fold and serve with chutney and sambar.

Mysore Pak – Karnataka

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup besan (gram flour)
  • 1 cup ghee, 1.5 cups sugar, 1/2 cup water

Cooking Process:

  1. Sift and roast besan in 1/4 cup ghee till aromatic.
  2. Make sugar syrup of 1 string consistency, add besan, stir continuously.
  3. Add remaining ghee in batches; cook till frothy and mixture leaves sides.
  4. Pour into greased tray, cool, cut into pieces.

Palak Paneer – North India

Ingredients:

  • 250g paneer, 3 cups spinach (palak)
  • 1 onion, 2 tomatoes, 2 green chilies, 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1/2 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp chili powder, salt

Cooking Process:

  1. Blanch spinach, blend to puree.
  2. Sauté onions, chilies, ginger garlic paste, add tomatoes and cook till mushy.
  3. Add spinach puree, spices, cook 5 min. Add paneer cubes, simmer 3-4 min.
  4. Serve hot with naan or rice.

Rogan Josh – Jammu & Kashmir

Ingredients:

  • 500g mutton/lamb
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 onions, 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • Whole spices: bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp fennel powder, salt

Cooking Process:

  1. Marinate mutton with yogurt, salt, and half the spices for 1 hour.
  2. Sauté whole spices and onions in oil, add ginger garlic paste.
  3. Add mutton, cook till browned. Add remaining spices, cook covered until meat is tender.
  4. Garnish and serve hot with steamed rice.

Khaman Dhokla – Gujarat

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup besan (gram flour)
  • 1 tbsp semolina (optional), 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Eno (fruit salt), 1/2 cup water
  • Salt, sugar, green chili, mustard seeds, curry leaves

Cooking Process:

  1. Mix besan, semolina, salt, lemon juice, water. Just before steaming, add Eno and mix well.
  2. Pour batter into greased tray, steam for 15-20 min.
  3. Temper with mustard seeds, curry leaves, green chili, pour over dhokla. Cut and serve.

Hyderabadi Biryani – Telangana

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups basmati rice, 500g chicken/mutton, 1 cup yogurt
  • Whole spices: bay leaf, cloves, cinnamon, cardamom
  • 2 onions, 2 tomatoes, mint, coriander, ginger garlic paste, saffron
  • Chili powder, garam masala, salt

Cooking Process:

  1. Marinate meat with yogurt, spices, mint, coriander for 2 hours.
  2. Parboil rice with whole spices.
  3. Layer meat and rice, top with fried onions, saffron milk.
  4. Cook on dum (sealed pot) for 25-30 min. Serve hot.

Machher Jhol (Bengali Fish Curry) – West Bengal

Ingredients:

  • 500g freshwater fish (Rohu/Katla)
  • 2 potatoes, 2 tomatoes, 1 tbsp ginger paste
  • Turmeric, red chili powder, cumin seeds, mustard oil, salt

Cooking Process:

  1. Marinate fish with salt & turmeric, shallow fry in mustard oil.
  2. Sauté cumin, ginger paste, sliced potatoes, tomatoes, spices.
  3. Add water, bring to boil, add fried fish, simmer till potatoes are cooked.
  4. Serve hot with rice.

Mishti Doi – West Bengal

Ingredients:

  • 1 liter milk, 1/2 cup sugar, 2 tbsp curd

Cooking Process:

  1. Boil and reduce milk to half, cool slightly.
  2. Caramelize sugar, mix into milk, cool to lukewarm.
  3. Add curd, set overnight in a warm place.
  4. Chill and serve.

Litti Chokha – Bihar

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour, 1 cup sattu (roasted gram flour)
  • Mustard oil, ajwain, kalonji, salt, pickling spices
  • Chokha: 2 eggplants, 2 tomatoes, 2 potatoes, green chili, onion, coriander

Cooking Process:

  1. Prepare dough with wheat flour, stuff with sattu mix, shape into balls.
  2. Bake/roast till golden. For chokha, roast veggies, peel, mash, mix with spices & onion.
  3. Serve litti with chokha and ghee.

Dal Baati Churma – Rajasthan

Ingredients:

  • For Baati: 2 cups wheat flour, 1/2 cup ghee, salt
  • For Dal: mixed lentils (toor, chana, moong), tomatoes, ginger, green chili, spice mix
  • For Churma: whole wheat flour, ghee, sugar

Cooking Process:

  2. Cook dal with spices, temper with ghee & cumin.
  3. For churma, crush baatis, mix with ghee & sugar.
  4. Serve dal, baati, and churma together with extra ghee.

Ghevar – Rajasthan

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour, 1/2 cup ghee, 1 cup chilled water
  • 1/2 cup milk, 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 2 cups sugar, 1 cup water, saffron, cardamom, nuts

Cooking Process:

  1. Make a thin batter. Pour batter in hot ghee in rings to form net structure.
  2. Fry till golden, drain. Dip in sugar syrup, top with saffron, nuts.

Ven Pongal – Tamil Nadu

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup rice, 1/4 cup moong dal
  • 2 tbsp ghee, 1 tsp black pepper, 1 tsp cumin, cashews, curry leaves, ginger

Cooking Process:

  2. In ghee, fry cumin, pepper, cashews, curry leaves, ginger; add to cooked rice-dal mixture.

Sambar – Tamil Nadu

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup toor dal (pigeon peas)
  • Mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, drumstick, potato)

Rasgulla – West Bengal

Ingredients:

  • 1 liter milk
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice/vinegar
  • 1 cup sugar, 4 cups water
  • Cardamom (optional)

Cooking Process:

  1. Boil milk, curdle with lemon juice, strain to get chenna (paneer).
  2. Knead chenna till smooth, shape into small balls.
  3. Boil sugar and water to make syrup, add chenna balls, cook till they expand and are cooked through.
  4. Cool and serve chilled.

Appam with Vegetable Stew – Kerala

Ingredients:

  • For Appam: 2 cups raw rice, 1/2 cup cooked rice, 1/2 cup grated coconut, 1/2 tsp yeast, 2 tbsp sugar, salt
  • For Stew: Mixed vegetables (carrot, potato, beans), 1 onion, 1 green chili, 1-inch ginger, 1 cup coconut milk, curry leaves, whole spices (cloves, cinnamon, cardamom), salt

Cooking Process:

  1. Soak raw rice for 4 hours. Grind with cooked rice and coconut to a smooth batter. Add yeast, sugar, salt, ferment overnight.
  2. For stew, sauté spices, onions, ginger, chili in oil. Add vegetables, cook till soft. Add coconut milk, simmer gently, season with salt.
  3. Make appams by pouring batter on a hot appam pan, swirling to spread thin edges, cook covered till done.
  4. Serve hot appams with vegetable stew.

Thekua – Bihar/Jharkhand

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1 cup jaggery (grated), 1/2 cup grated coconut
  • 2 tbsp ghee, 1/2 tsp cardamom powder, water, oil for frying

Cooking Process:

  1. Melt jaggery in water, cool. Mix flour, coconut, cardamom, ghee, and jaggery water to make a stiff dough.
  2. Shape into discs, press with design, deep fry till golden brown.
  3. Cool and store in airtight container.

Amritsari Kulcha – Punjab

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour, 1/2 cup yogurt, 1/2 tsp baking powder, salt
  • Stuffing: 2 boiled potatoes, 1 onion, green chili, coriander, spices
  • Butter/ghee for brushing

Cooking Process:

  1. Knead dough with flour, yogurt, baking powder, salt. Rest 2 hours.
  2. Prepare stuffing with mashed potatoes, onion, chili, coriander, spices.
  3. Stuff dough balls, roll, cook on tawa or bake till golden. Brush with butter.
  4. Serve hot with chole or raita.

Samosa – All India (Fast Food)

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour, 4 tbsp oil, salt, water
  • Stuffing: 4 boiled potatoes, 1/2 cup peas, ginger, green chili, spices (coriander, cumin, garam masala, chili powder)
  • Oil for frying

Cooking Process:

  1. Knead stiff dough with flour, oil, salt, water. Rest 30 min.
  2. Prepare stuffing with mashed potatoes, peas, spices.
  3. Roll dough, cut, fill with stuffing, shape into cones, seal edges.
  4. Deep fry till golden and crisp. Serve with chutney.

Pav Bhaji – Fast Food (Popular in North India)

Ingredients:

  • 4 pav buns, 2 potatoes, 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrot, cauliflower), 1 onion, 2 tomatoes

Cooking Process:

  1. Boil and mash vegetables. Sauté onions, tomatoes, add veggies, pav bhaji masala, salt, butter. Cook till thick.

  3. Serve bhaji with pav, lemon, and chopped onions.

Masala Chai (Tea) – All India (Fast Food)

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups water, 1 cup milk
  • 2 tsp black tea leaves
  • 2-3 tsp sugar (to taste)
  • Spices: 2 green cardamoms, 1-inch ginger, 2 cloves, 1 small cinnamon stick, 4 black peppercorns

Cooking Process:

  1. Crush spices and ginger. Boil water with spices for 2-3 min.
  2. Add tea leaves, simmer 2 min. Add milk and sugar, boil for 2-3 min.
  3. Strain and serve hot.

Maggi Masala Noodles – All India (Fast Food)

Ingredients:

  • 1 pack Maggi noodles (or instant noodles)
  • 1.5 cups water
  • 1 small onion, 1 small tomato, 1 green chili (optional)
  • 1/4 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrot, capsicum, optional)

Cooking Process:

  1. Heat oil, sauté onion, chili, tomato, and vegetables till soft.
  2. Add water, bring to boil. Add noodles and tastemaker.
  3. Cook till water is absorbed and noodles are soft. Serve hot.
